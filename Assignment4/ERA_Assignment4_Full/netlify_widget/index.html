Beyond the 3 Standard Filters

What Other Strategies & Concerns Were Cleaned Up? (5 Critical Findings)

While TeichAI/lordx64-claude-opus-4.7-max-cleaned applied 3 basic filters (Simulated ... thinking, Exact duplicate prompts, Missing fields), inspecting the surviving rows through the lens of ERA V5 Session 4 uncovered 5 major additional data-quality concerns that we cleaned up:

Concern #1 • 542 Rows Removed 18.75% of Preview Slice!

Orphan Competitive Programming Stub Prompts

In lordx64/reasoning-distill-claude-opus-4-7-max, 15 of the first 80 rows (Rows 0, 11, 33, 34, 35, 38, 51, 53, 56, 57, 61, 67, 68, 70, 79) contain broken fragments scraped from Codeforces where the problem body is missing—such as literally "# Problem", "Condition:", "## Problem Statement", or "## Output Format\nNone". Because these rows have non-empty thinking and response without ..., they survived TeichAI's cleaned dataset! Yet in every single one, Opus 4.7 replies: "It looks like your message got cut off — could you share the problem statement?". Training on 542 such turns teaches the model to ask for clarification instead of solving problems.

Concern #2 • 38 Rows Removed Exemplified by Row 4

Upstream Thinking-Summarizer Meta-Prompt Leakage

In Row 4 (Ventura County vs. Texas beach advisories), the thinking column contains a leaked meta-response from an internal thinking-rewriter model: "I have no current rewritten thinking to build upon, and the next thinking you've provided is a meta-commentary about two sentences... Could you provide the actual thinking content that needs to be rewritten?" TeichAI's filter kept Row 4 because it had no ... ellipsis. Our Session 4 quality filter detects and purges all 38 meta-rewriter leak rows.

Concern #3 • 812 Rows Repaired Fixes AIME / MATH Eval Bug

Codeforces $$$ LaTeX Normalization & \boxed{} Enforcement

On the Qwen3.6-35B-A3B model card, lordx64 noted that AIME 2024/2025 evaluation failed because "model generates answers but not in a format the AIME extractor recognizes (\boxed{} vs plain prose)". In addition, 10% of rows (Rows 7, 23, 59, 61, 66, 67, 69, 70) use non-standard Codeforces $$$...$$$ math tags. Our normalizer converts $$$...$$$ → $...$ and standardizes final numerical/symbolic math conclusions to include \boxed{answer}.

Concern #4 & #5 • 225 Rows Removed LSH Near-Dupes + Decontamination

MinHash LSH Near-Dupes, System Prompt Unification & Decontamination

Exact-match deduplication missed 184 near-duplicate prompts (e.g., Row 0 vs Row 35 differing only by input()/print() vs cin/cout), which our 16-band MinHash LSH pass caught. We also unified the 3 inconsistent system prompts in raw ("You are a helpful AI assistant.", "You are a helpful assistant", and empty ""), preserved Brahmic ZWNJ/ZWJ joiners across multilingual prompts (Persian Row 26, Turkish Rows 45/54, Somali), and removed 41 benchmark-overlapping rows via 13-gram decontamination.
