Apple MPS is visible to this PyTorch build, but the assignment suite is pinned to CPU in configs/default.yaml for a deterministic, GPU-free grade path. Training precision for the measured runs is therefore FP32. MPS FP16/BF16 was not benchmarked and is not recommended on the basis of the 0.1 encoding error alone. CUDA BF16 mixed precision is the usual large-model default, and it is not available here.
