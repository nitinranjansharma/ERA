# Cross-lane requirements

S3 stated requirements that S5 has no lane for. They are tags or sourcing problems, not extra percentages.

**India-first as a lane** at 9% of 2.91T = 262B against ~8B of open English India-first text (court judgments, if ingested) = 31 epochs. Unfundable as a lane. As a *tag* on Indic + English-India documents it is over-delivered if even half the Indic lane is India-context (φ ≥ 0.5). φ is not measured in this repo; the coverage claim is conditional on that.

**≥15% of STEM in Indic languages** needs 52B against ~12B of Indic-language STEM in the open inventory (~3.4%). 4.5× short. No reallocation substitutes. The sourcing dependency is NCERT/SCERT/NPTEL/IGNOU (gated). Decision: prioritise that negotiation; fallback is to lower the requirement to 3.4% and concede the capability.

**≥55% of India-first non-English** passes at every φ in the 0.5–1.0 range because the Indic lane is already 17%.
